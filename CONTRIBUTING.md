# Contributing

Vigiver is an open-source project exploring blockchain security, cryptographic resilience, and verifiable autonomous agents.

You are welcome to explore the repository, experiment with its ideas, challenge its assumptions, and build your own interpretation of the project.

## Fork the project

You do not need permission to experiment.

Fork the repository, change its architecture, explore alternative cryptographic mechanisms, integrate other blockchain networks, or build something entirely different from the original implementation, subject to the applicable license terms.

Independent experimentation is encouraged.

## Share ideas

Contributions can take many forms:

- Proposing alternative approaches.
- Identifying incorrect assumptions.
- Improving documentation or accessibility.
- Reproducing security findings.
- Adding tests or blockchain integrations.
- Sharing experimental implementations.

A contribution does not need to be large or production-ready to start a useful conversation.

## Contribute upstream

If you would like to integrate a change into this repository, explain what you are changing and why.

Focused pull requests are easier to understand and review. The project uses [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/en/v1.0.0/) to maintain a readable development history, but you do not need to master every convention before participating.

Please avoid committing private keys, credentials, personal information, or sensitive vulnerability details.

Security-sensitive findings should be communicated responsibly.

## Keep exploring

This project is experimental. Its ideas and implementation will evolve.

Question the design, verify its claims, and build on the parts you find useful.
